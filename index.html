DesignScout / DSS
Internal research application
Product discovery & market intelligence

Research-driven product ideas for embroidered apparel.

DesignScout is an internal tool that helps an apparel team research publicly available marketplace listings, assess seasonal patterns, and plan embroidery product concepts.

Marketplace research

Examine permitted public listing details such as titles, prices, and listing metadata.

Seasonal opportunities

Organize seasonal events and observed signals to support product planning.

Internal analysis

Compare research observations over time for informed, human-reviewed decisions.

How DesignScout uses Etsy's API

DesignScout is being developed to access Etsy's official Open API only as authorized. Its planned Etsy integration searches or retrieves permitted publicly available listing information for internal market research and analysis.

DesignScout does not place orders, upload or edit listings, or access private seller account data. We do not claim access to verified sales figures from public listings. API usage will comply with Etsy's applicable developer terms, permissions, and rate limits.

Who uses DesignScout?

DesignScout is intended for internal use by a small apparel research and product development team. It is not currently offered as a public or paid software service.

Development status

DesignScout is an early-stage internal application. Integrations and research features are being developed and tested; availability may change as required permissions are approved.
